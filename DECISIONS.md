# DECISIONS.md

A lightweight record of important product, design, and engineering decisions made while developing Portofoliov2.

## Decision 001 — Portfolio as a Product

**Decision:** Treat the portfolio as a digital product rather than a static project gallery.

**Why:** The goal is to communicate both finished work and the way the designer/developer thinks, designs, and builds.

## Decision 002 — UI/UX × Front-end Positioning

**Decision:** Present UI/UX design and front-end development as two connected disciplines.

**Why:** The portfolio is intended to demonstrate the ability to move from interface thinking to working implementation.

## Decision 003 — Editorial Visual Direction

**Decision:** Use a minimal editorial foundation with Japanese-inspired visual language.

**Why:** Editorial hierarchy, whitespace, typography, and restrained motion create a distinctive identity without relying on excessive decoration.

## Decision 004 — Motion With Purpose

**Decision:** Keep interaction motion subtle and functional.

**Why:** Animation should establish hierarchy, reveal content, communicate state, and add depth without becoming the main attraction.

## Decision 005 — Accessibility Is Part of the Experience

**Decision:** Account for reduced motion, responsive layouts, keyboard interaction, and semantic structure.

**Why:** A visually expressive portfolio should remain usable for different users and input methods.

## Decision 006 — Reuse Before Reinventing

**Decision:** Extend existing components and patterns before creating new abstractions.

**Why:** This keeps the codebase maintainable and prevents visual inconsistencies across the portfolio.
