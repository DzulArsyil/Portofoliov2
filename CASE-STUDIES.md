# CASE-STUDIES.md

## Purpose

This file defines the structure used to document selected portfolio projects so that each case study communicates process, reasoning, outcome, and implementation—not only the final visual.

## Case Study Structure

### 01 — Context

- What is the project?
- Who is it for?
- What was the initial situation?

### 02 — Problem

Describe the user, business, or product problem being addressed.

### 03 — Goals

List the measurable or observable goals of the project.

### 04 — Discovery

Document relevant research, observations, assumptions, constraints, and references.

### 05 — Design Direction

Explain the visual and interaction direction, including the reasoning behind major design choices.

### 06 — Solution

Show how the final product addresses the identified problem.

### 07 — Interaction

Describe meaningful interactions, motion, navigation, responsive behavior, and accessibility considerations.

### 08 — Development

Explain the technology choices and how the design was translated into a working interface.

### 09 — Outcome

Describe what was delivered and what was learned. Use concrete evidence when available.

### 10 — Reflection

Record what would be improved in a future iteration.

---

## Writing Principles

- Focus on decisions and reasoning, not only screenshots.
- Distinguish facts from assumptions.
- Show constraints and trade-offs honestly.
- Keep technical details relevant to the design story.
- Use concise, outcome-oriented language.
