# Product Requirements Document — Portofoliov2

## 1. Product Overview

**Portofoliov2** is a personal portfolio experience for **M. Dzul'Arsyil Aziz**, positioned at the intersection of UI/UX design, front-end development, and creative technology.

The portfolio should communicate not only what has been built, but also how the designer/developer thinks, designs, and implements digital experiences.

## 2. Primary Goal

Create a memorable portfolio that helps visitors quickly understand:

- Who Dzul'Arsyil is
- What he specializes in
- What projects he has worked on
- How he approaches design and development
- How to explore or contact him

## 3. Target Audience

### Primary

- Recruiters
- Hiring managers
- Potential clients
- Design and development collaborators

### Secondary

- Other designers and developers
- Students and creative technologists

## 4. Core Content

- Personal introduction
- Featured projects
- Design direction
- Design and development toolkit
- Experience
- Technology used to build the portfolio
- Accessibility and interaction considerations
- Contact links

## 5. Experience Principles

### Clarity First

Visitors should understand the portfolio's purpose without excessive exploration.

### Design × Code

The interface should demonstrate both visual design ability and front-end implementation quality.

### Motion With Purpose

Animation should reinforce hierarchy, transitions, and atmosphere rather than become visual noise.

### Responsive By Default

Important content and interactions must remain usable across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

### Authenticity

The portfolio should feel personal rather than like a generic portfolio template.

## 6. Success Criteria

The portfolio is successful when a first-time visitor can answer these questions quickly:

1. Who is Dzul'Arsyil?
2. Is he a UI/UX Designer, Front-end Developer, or both?
3. What has he built?
4. What makes his approach distinctive?
5. How can I contact him?

## 7. Non-Goals

- Building a generic design-template website
- Adding animation without a UX purpose
- Prioritizing visual effects over readability
- Showing every experiment instead of curating selected work
